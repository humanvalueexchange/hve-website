# HVE V2 Content Audit

## Sources reviewed

- Current website: https://humanvalueexchange.com
- Current About page: https://humanvalueexchange.com/about/
- Current Workshops page: https://humanvalueexchange.com/workshops/
- Legacy Square site: https://humanvalueexchange.square.site/
- Legacy About page: https://humanvalueexchange.square.site/about/
- Legacy Philosophy page: https://humanvalueexchange.square.site/philosophy/
- Legacy Contact page: https://humanvalueexchange.square.site/contact/

The legacy Square site is a JavaScript-rendered site. Its most useful content was available in the published page data, especially on the About and Philosophy pages. The audit preserves ideas and positioning, not the Square layout or copy verbatim.

## 1. Current site strengths

### Clear central idea

The current homepage leads with a strong, memorable promise:

> Wealth is more than money.

That statement gives HVE a clear category distinction and creates a natural bridge from financial wealth to the other four pillars.

### Coherent framework

The Five Types of Wealth are consistently named and explained:

1. Time Wealth
2. Physical Wealth
3. Mental Wealth
4. Social Wealth
5. Financial Wealth

The individual wealth pages already give each pillar a concise definition, practical framing, and a route toward workshops.

### Strong brand language

The current site uses a focused set of meaningful ideas:

- Freedom
- Sovereignty
- Self-mastery
- Human flourishing
- Intentional investment
- A life rich in every direction

This is a strong foundation for a premium consulting and education brand.

### Simple conversion path

The homepage and Workshops page already move visitors toward:

1. Understanding the framework
2. Exploring a workshop
3. Booking a discovery call

The V2 opportunity is to make this path more specific for different audiences and services.

### Technical and visual foundation

The current site is lightweight, static-export compatible, responsive, and built around a restrained dark green, cream, sage, and gold palette. That supports the V2 direction of simplicity, clarity, and trust.

## 2. Legacy Square content worth preserving

### Mission and positioning

The legacy site describes HVE as helping people achieve “freedom and sovereignty” through a combined approach to:

- Wellness
- Mindfulness
- Wealth education
- Practical lifestyle change

This expands the current framework from an idea into a method: HVE helps people improve the conditions that make freedom possible.

Recommended V2 wording:

> Human Value Exchange helps individuals, families, and builders develop the time, health, clarity, relationships, and financial capacity required for a freer life.

### Founder story

The strongest legacy material is the father-son story:

- Hans and Wolfgang Westphal are presented as co-founders.
- Hans contributes strategy, technology, AI productivity, mindfulness, wealth systems, and long-range vision.
- Wolfgang contributes practical coaching, physical resilience, discipline, and daily execution.
- Together they represent a bridge between long-term perspective and hands-on change.

Recommended V2 angle:

> HVE is a father-son project built around a shared question: what would it take to help people build a life that is strong, free, and fully their own?

The founder page should make the relationship and complementary strengths tangible without repeating every biographical or spiritual detail from the legacy site.

### Individuals and families

The legacy site explicitly speaks to individuals and families seeking integrated growth, family legacy, and a more intentional future. This audience language is valuable because the current site is currently broad but impersonal.

Recommended audience language:

> HVE serves individuals, families, entrepreneurs, professionals, coaches, advisors, and small business owners who want a more complete way to think about wealth and wellbeing.

### Wellness and embodied change

The legacy site includes concrete wellness language that is more useful than generic “holistic” positioning:

- Lose fat
- Build muscle
- Improve physical resilience
- Create sustainable habits
- Use the body as the foundation for daily capability

This should become a practical Physical Wealth and coaching offer, with careful claims and no implication that HVE provides medical treatment.

### Coaching and consulting

The legacy material points toward several service formats:

- One-on-one coaching
- Lifestyle consulting
- Sovereignty assessments
- Workshops
- Meditation or mindfulness journeys
- Integrated playbooks

For V2, these should be organized into a small number of clear offers rather than presented as an unstructured list.

### Financial education and sovereignty

The legacy site connects financial sovereignty with:

- Understanding fiat systems
- Building financial literacy
- Treating money as a tool for stability and choice
- Exploring Bitcoin as a long-term sovereignty asset
- Building family legacy through better financial decisions

This is relevant to HVE, but the V2 site should present it as education and decision support, not investment advice or a promise of financial outcomes.

Recommended wording:

> Financial sovereignty starts with understanding the systems that shape your choices, using money intentionally, and building enough resilience to protect your time and values. HVE offers education—not individualized investment advice.

### Education and practical integration

The legacy site’s most useful service promise is integration: body, mind, relationships, and money should reinforce one another rather than be optimized in isolation.

Recommended V2 wording:

> We turn reflection into practical experiments, repeatable habits, and decisions that compound across the five forms of wealth.

### Contact and work-with-us intent

The legacy navigation includes About, Philosophy, Programs, Blog, and Contact. The current site has About, The Five Types, Workshops, Blog, and Work with us, but no dedicated contact or services page.

The V2 site should make the next step explicit:

- Book a discovery call
- Ask about a workshop
- Explore coaching or consulting
- Subscribe to the newsletter

## 3. Content that should be rewritten

### Ground the mystical language

The legacy site uses phrases such as “mystery school,” “cosmic alignment,” “Creator,” “divine communion,” “saintly presence,” and “1000 years of peace.” These may reflect personal beliefs, but they are too esoteric or expansive for the primary HVE marketing site.

Rewrite them as:

- Reflection and mindfulness
- Values-based living
- Attention and emotional regulation
- Physical vitality
- Financial education
- Long-term stewardship
- Human flourishing

Personal spiritual practice can remain part of a founder story if clearly framed as personal context rather than a universal claim.

### Replace overclaims with credible outcomes

Claims about awakening genius, manifesting abundance, cosmic alignment, or creating an equitable global financial system should become specific, supportable outcomes:

- Clarify priorities
- Assess current strengths and constraints
- Build sustainable habits
- Improve decision quality
- Create more time and financial resilience
- Align work, health, and relationships with values

### Make Bitcoin language educational

The legacy site presents Bitcoin as “sound money” and a sovereignty asset. HVE can retain the topic, but should avoid prescriptive or guaranteed language.

Use:

> Bitcoin, monetary systems, and financial sovereignty are subjects for education and informed conversation within the Financial Wealth pillar.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed appreciation
- “Escape” or “salvation” narratives
- Direct buy or sell recommendations
- Implying that one asset solves financial insecurity

### Simplify service language

“Full integrated playbooks” and several overlapping program names are difficult to understand quickly. V2 should use plain service categories:

- Assessments
- Coaching
- Consulting
- Workshops
- Education

Each offer should state who it is for, what happens, and what the next step is.

### Clarify founder roles

The legacy biographies contain valuable detail but are too long and mix professional, spiritual, and promotional language. V2 should lead with:

- Who Hans and Wolfgang are
- Why they built HVE
- What each brings
- How their work helps clients

Longer personal background can move to expandable sections or the blog.

## 4. Pages that need improvement

### Homepage

Keep the “wealth is more than money” opening, then add:

- The five pillars in plain language
- A clear audience statement
- A founder or origin-story section
- A concise services overview
- Blog/newsletter invitation
- A stronger discovery-call CTA

### About / Founders

Expand the current About page into a founder story featuring Hans and Wolfgang, the father-son origin, complementary strengths, and the practical reason HVE exists.

### Five Types of Wealth

Keep the existing five pages, but add for each pillar:

- What it means
- Signs it is underdeveloped
- What improvement looks like
- Example practices or decisions
- Relevant HVE service or workshop

### Services

Create a dedicated Services page covering assessments, coaching, consulting, education, and workshops. This is currently the largest information gap between the legacy positioning and the V1 site.

### Workshops

Expand beyond a general description. Add workshop themes, intended audiences, delivery formats, expected outcomes, and a clear booking path.

### Blog integration

The blog domain is linked, but the main site should explain what readers will find there:

- Five Types of Wealth essays
- Founder perspectives
- Practical experiments
- Financial sovereignty education
- Wellness and self-mastery

### Contact / Work with us

Create a focused contact page with separate paths for individuals, families, teams, and partners. Keep the discovery-call CTA prominent and include a concise expectation-setting statement.

### HVE Life OS

Add a future-vision section or page describing HVE Life OS as a future personal operating system for measuring and improving all five forms of wealth. Do not add application functionality in this phase.

| Contact | Capture qualified interest | Individual, family, team, partner, and general inquiry paths | Book a discovery call |
| HVE Life OS | Frame the future vision | Personal operating system for five-wealth measurement and improvement | Follow the journey |

### Suggested funnel

Visitor  
→ Five Types overview  
→ Relevant pillar or blog article  
→ Assessment, workshop, or discovery call  
→ Coaching / consulting relationship  
→ Future HVE Life OS ecosystem

### Editorial guardrails

- Keep “wealth is more than money” as the central idea.
- Use freedom and sovereignty as outcomes supported by practical action.
- Treat wellness, mindfulness, Bitcoin, and financial systems as educational topics.
- Lead with individuals and families, then broaden to professionals, entrepreneurs, teams, and partners.
- Prefer specific, credible language over spiritual or transformational overclaims.
- Preserve the current visual simplicity; improve depth through content structure, not visual complexity.
